# ArtRamp

*Create more. Miss nothing.*

Interactive prototype: **https://azadekayadesign-gif.github.io/ArtRamp/**

ArtRamp helps independent visual artists evaluate art opportunities (open calls, grants, residencies) and turn their requirements into a weekly application plan. BrainStation Product Design final project.

- Works on phone and desktop, no sign-in.
- AI extraction and task suggestions are simulated with fixed rules (no API).
- Three real opportunities (details checked 7 Nov 2026) plus one fictional practice call for usability testing.
